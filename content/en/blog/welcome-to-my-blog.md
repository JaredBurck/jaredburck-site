Welcome to my blog! I'm excited to share my journey of creating this website and the experiences that led me here. As a managing architect and consultant with a passion for software development, I wanted a platform that could effectively showcase both my technical expertise and professional experience.

## Why This Website?

After exploring various options, I chose to build this site using Hugo, a powerful static site generator, and used a theme called CareerCanvas (created by Felipe Cordero). This decision was driven by several factors:

- **Performance**: Hugo's static site generation ensures lightning-fast loading times
- **Flexibility**: The ability to create custom layouts and components
- **Developer Experience**: Writing content in Markdown makes it easy to focus on what matters
- **Modern Design**: A clean, responsive interface that works beautifully on all devices

## What to Expect

In this blog, I'll be sharing insights about:

- **Consulting Best Practices and Lessons Learned**: My experiences and projects in the field
- **Software Development**: Tips and tricks for administrators and engineers learning to code
- **Application Modernization**: The latest developments and applications
- **Automation**: Streamlining manaul and automated processes
- **Latest AI Technologies**: Reviews and tutorials of useful tools

## The Tech Stack

This website is built with:

- **Hugo**: For static site generation
- **Tailwind CSS**: For modern, utility-first styling
- **CareerCanvas**: Theme designed by Felipe Cordero for technical professionals
- **GitHub**: For version control and deployment (GitHub Actions configured but deployment not used today)
- **CloudFlare**: For domain registrar and deployment

## Join Me on This Journey

I'll be sharing not just technical content, but also my experiences in:

- Creating and maintaining this website

- Learning and implementing new technologies
- Balancing engineering and software development
- Creative or interesting non-technical passions, adventures, or other content relevant to me

Stay tuned for more content coming soon! I'm particularly excited to share all my experiences with consulting and how it can help other technical professionals showcase their work effectively.
